# GTA San Andreas  Restart Mission Mod

A mod that allows you to restart a mission if you fail it. It works like this: after failing a mission, you are given the option to restart. If you choose yes, you are teleported to the mission marker, and your health, armor, ammo, and wanted level are restored to the values ​​you had when you stepped on the marker; the weather and time of day are also reset to what they were at that moment. The mod also supports controllers.

If you encounter any issues or instability, feel free to contribute or even create your own version if you can improve upon this one. Sorry for any problem.
